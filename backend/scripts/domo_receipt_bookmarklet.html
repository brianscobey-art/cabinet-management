Pulls the DOMO PO Receipt List with no access token — your login is the credential.

Drag this button up to your bookmarks bar:

Pull PO Receipts

Press Ctrl+Shift+B first if the bookmarks bar is hidden.

Using it

  1. Be signed in at carterlumber.domo.com — any page.
  2. Click Pull PO Receipts in your bookmarks bar.
  3. A green button appears in the middle of the page. Click it.

That is the whole job. The file saves to your Downloads folder, the upload task ships it within 5 minutes, and cabinettron.com imports it on its own.

Why the second click? Chrome silently discards a download that a page starts by itself — no error, no file. Only a real click counts. The bookmarklet can't do that part for you, but it does tell you how many receipts it found, so you know it worked before you click.

Nothing happens? You're signed out of DOMO — sign in and click again.
